Scratch tutorial for kids · 6 steps · about 1 hour

Build Comet Rally

In Comet Rally you play against a robot. You both have a paddle. Hit the glowing ball back, and try to get it past the robot. The first one to 5 points wins!

You need the Scratch editor: scratch.mit.edu/projects/editor. Every step has a file you can download if you get stuck.

Your paddle

First, get the pictures. Download the starter project, then open it in Scratch with File › Load from your computer.

The starter project has the court, the two paddles and the ball. No code yet.

Download starter.sb3

Your paddle is the blue one, called Player. Click it, and build this script:

Player new
  • go to x: -210 y: 0 puts the paddle on the left side.
  • forever repeats the blocks inside it, again and again, until you stop the game.
  • set y to (mouse y) moves the paddle up and down to where your mouse is. y means up and down.

The ball starts by pointing at 45: halfway between up and right. Click the Ball and build this:

Ball new
  • move 6 steps moves the ball forward a little, in the direction it points.
  • if on edge, bounce turns the ball around when it hits a wall, just like a real ball.
  • Both are inside forever, so the ball keeps on flying.

3

Hit it!

This is the cleverest part of the game. When the ball hits your paddle, it should bounce back. And where it hits your paddle decides where it goes:

The middle of your paddle sends the ball straight across. The top sends it up. The bottom sends it down. That is how you aim!

The aim block

The ball works out how far it is from the middle of your paddle: its own y position, minus the paddle’s y position. Then it turns that far away from straight (90), times 1.2:

Where the ball hitsDistance from the middleNew directionThe ball goes
The middle090 − 0 = 90Straight across
Near the top2090 − 24 = 66Up and right
Near the bottom−2090 + 24 = 114Down and right

Add this if block to the Ball’s script, right after move 6 steps. You find ([y position] of (Player)) in Sensing: pick Player in the second menu and y position in the first.

Ball changed
  • touching Player? is true when the ball touches your paddle.
  • direction < 0 means “flying to the left”, towards your paddle. Without it, the ball could get stuck on the paddle and bounce again and again.

Try it

Hit the ball with the middle of your paddle, then with the top, then with the bottom. See how it flies off differently?

Try this

  • Change 1.2 to 2. Now the aim is much stronger. Is that too wild?

The robot

Time for an opponent! The red paddle on the right is the Robot. It watches the ball and moves towards it, but only a little bit each time, so it is a bit slow. That way you can beat it.

The Robot gets a script, and the Ball gets a second if block, just like the one for your paddle, but mirrored:

Robot new
Ball changed
  • The Robot finds how far it is from the ball, and moves one tenth of the way each time. When the ball is far away it moves fast; when it is close, it slows down.
  • The Ball bounces off the Robot with the same aim trick. The robot sends the ball to the left, so it starts from −90 (left) instead of 90.
  • direction > 0 means “flying to the right”, towards the robot.

Try it

Play a rally with the robot. Can you make it miss by aiming steeply?

Try this

Points

When the ball gets past a paddle, the other side scores a point. Then the ball goes back to the middle and is served again.

  1. In Variables, click Make a Variable and make Your score. Then make another one called Robot score.
  2. Add the new blocks to the Ball’s script. The two new if blocks go just before if on edge, bounce.
Ball changed
  • x position < -220 means the ball got past your paddle on the left. The robot gets a point.
  • x position > 220 means it got past the robot on the right. You get a point!
  • pick random serves the ball in a slightly different direction each time, so no two serves are the same. It is served towards whoever just lost the point.
  • wait 1 seconds gives everyone a moment to get ready.

6

Make it awesome

The last step adds three fun things:

  • Speed: every time someone hits the ball, it gets a bit faster. Make a variable called speed, and use it in move (speed) steps.
  • Colours: every hit changes the ball’s colour with the color effect.
  • A winner: the first to 5 points wins, and the ball says who won.

Your paddle also gets two if blocks, so it can’t slide off the top or bottom of the court.
