Scratch tutorial for kids (8+) · 4 short steps · basic blocks only

Sky Guard

Meteors are falling on your valley! Turn the cannon with the mouse, click to shoot, and don't let three of them land. The whole game is 43 lines of blocks, with no custom blocks and no lists.

Every step is a game you can play, and every step has a file you can download if you get stuck. You need the Scratch editor: scratch.mit.edu/projects/editor.

Start here: the starter project has the cannon, the bullet, the meteor and the night sky. No code yet.

Aim

Big idea: point towardsA sprite always points somewhere: that's its direction. One block, point towards (mouse-pointer), turns the cannon to face the mouse. Put it in a forever loop and the cannon follows your hand.

Make: nothing new yet. The starter has the Cannon, Bullet and Meteor sprites and the night sky.

Cannon new
  • The cannon costume is drawn pointing right, because in Scratch direction 90 means right. The little circle at its back is the turning point.
  • Try point towards with other things later: a bullet pointing towards a meteor would be a homing missile!

Shoot

Big idea: direction + move steps = speedA bullet doesn't need x and y speeds. It just copies the cannon's direction and moves 12 steps every frame. That is how Scratch likes to move things: point, then move.

Make: the variable cool (for all sprites).

Cannon changed
Bullet new
Bullet new
  • A click makes a clone of the Bullet. The clone goes to the cannon, points the same way (direction of Cannon) and flies until it touches the edge. Then it deletes itself.
  • cool counts down from 8 after each shot, so holding the mouse shoots one bullet at a time instead of a stream.
  • Space shoots too, which is handy on a laptop.

Meteors

Big idea: clones from the skyThe Meteor sprite never shows itself. It just waits a random time, jumps to a random spot above the screen and makes a clone. Each clone falls by itself: change y by -2, again and again.

Make: the variable score.

Cannon changed
Bullet changed
Meteor new
Meteor new
  • A meteor that touches a Bullet adds 1 to the score and deletes itself. The bullet stops when it touches a meteor and waits a tiny moment before it goes, so the meteor has time to notice. Without that wait, the bullet could disappear first and the meteor would survive!
  • A meteor that gets below y = -140 has landed. It just deletes itself, for now.
  • pick random 0.6 to 1.6 seconds between meteors: try 0.3 for a storm.

Lives and waves

Big idea: one number makes it harderThree lives: a meteor that lands takes one. And the meteors' speed is no longer 2, it is 2 + score ÷ 10: after ten hits they fall at 3, after twenty at 4. That one line is the whole difficulty curve.

Make: the variables lives and speed.

Cannon changed
Meteor changed
  • The meteor clone uses change y by (0 − speed). Every meteor reads speed each frame, so the ones already falling speed up too.
  • When lives reaches 0 the cannon says Game over for 2 seconds and then stop all. Say it first: stop all also clears speech bubbles.
  • This is the whole game: 43 lines of blocks!

Make it your own

  • Bouncing bullets: add if on edge, bounce inside the bullet's loop and let it fly for 2 seconds instead of until the edge.
  • Big meteors: some clones do set size to 160% and need two hits (give each clone its own hits variable).
  • A homing bullet: point towards Meteor every frame instead of flying straight. Too easy? Make it slower.
  • Change 2 + score ÷ 10. What happens with ÷ 5? With ÷ 20?
