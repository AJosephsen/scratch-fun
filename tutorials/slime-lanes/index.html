Slime Lanes

Slimes are crawling in from the right along four lanes towards your castle! Drag crystal towers onto the field to stop them. Destroyed slimes give coins for more towers, repairs and upgrades: faster shooting, stronger bolts, bolts that fly through, and sturdier towers.

Every step is a game you can play, and every step has a file you can download if you get stuck. You need the Scratch editor: scratch.mit.edu/projects/editor.

Start here: the starter project has the slimes, the tower, the tools and the field. No code yet.

Slimes

Big idea: clones on lanesThe Slime sprite hides and, every few seconds, picks one of four lanes and makes a clone of itself there. The lanes are 60 apart: set y to 90 − 60 × pick random 0 to 3. Each clone starts at the right and crawls left until it reaches the castle wall.

Make: nothing new yet. The starter has the Slime, Tower, Tool and Ring sprites and the field.

Towers

Big idea: drag and snapClick the tower in the shop and a new one appears under your mouse. Its clone script says repeat until not mouse down: go to mouse-pointer: it follows you until you let go. Then it snaps to the nearest square with round.

Make: the variable coins, and role, hp, maxhp, delay, power and pierce for this sprite only.

Shooting

Big idea: bolts are clones of their towerEvery 0.7 seconds a tower turns into a bolt for a moment: set role to bolt, bolt costume, create clone of myself, then back to a tower. The clone starts right where its tower is and flies right along the lane, towards the slimes.

Make: nothing new.

Tower changed
  • Why a clone of the tower and not of a Bolt sprite? A clone copies its parent: its place, and its own variables. Later the bolts will copy their tower's upgrades this way.

Hits and coins

Big idea: touching colorA slime that touches the bolts' bright yellow loses 1 health. At 0 it is gone and you get 10 coins. A slime that gets through the castle wall costs one of your 5 lives.

Make: the variable lives, and health and ouch for the Slime only.

Upgrades

Big idea: a tool, then a towerFour more tools: Speed (shoots twice as often), Damage (red bolts that take 2 health), Pierce (bolts fly through slimes) and Strength (double health). Pick a tool, then click a tower: only that tower gets the upgrade.

Make: nothing new: the upgrades change the tower's own variables.
