# Scratch Fun

Step-by-step Scratch tutorials for kids, and finished games to download and remix in the Scratch editor
([scratch.mit.edu/projects/editor](https://scratch.mit.edu/projects/editor/), **File › Load from your computer**).
Open `index.html` for the overview.

## Tutorials

| Tutorial | Level | About |
|---|---|---|
| [Sky Guard](tutorials/sky-guard/index.html) | Scratch tutorial for kids (8+) · 4 short steps · basic blocks only | Meteors are falling on your valley! Turn the cannon with the mouse, click to shoot, and don't let three of them land. The whole game is 43 lines of blocks, with no custom blocks and no lists. |
| [Sheepdog](tutorials/sheepdog/index.html) | Scratch tutorial for kids (8+) · 4 short steps · basic blocks only | You are the dog. The sheep are scared of you, so wherever you run, they run the other way. Use that to herd all eight into the pen, as fast as you can. The whole game is 28 lines of blocks. |
| [Maze Maker](tutorials/maze-maker/index.html) | Scratch tutorial for kids · 4 short steps | A little mole digs tunnels through the soil, and when it is done, there is a maze. Every time you click the green flag, you get a new one. The trick that makes it work is a block that uses itself! |
| [Splat Patrol](tutorials/splat-patrol/index.html) | Scratch tutorial for kids (8+) · 5 steps · basic blocks only | Sneaky raccoon bandits are hiding in the houses, ready to throw water balloons. Windows pop open: splat the raccoons before they splat you, but don't splash the granny or the kid! Round after round the houses change, and the raccoons get quicker. |
| [Beanstalk Bounce](tutorials/beanstalk-bounce/index.html) | Scratch tutorial for kids (9+) · 5 steps | A frog bounces up a giant beanstalk, from leaf to leaf. The higher he gets, the further apart the leaves are, and the more tricks they have: moving leaves, crumbling leaves, springy mushrooms, and bees. How high can you climb? |
| [Build Comet Rally](tutorials/comet-rally/index.html) | Scratch tutorial for kids · 6 steps · about 1 hour | In Comet Rally you play against a robot. You both have a paddle. Hit the glowing ball back, and try to get it past the robot. The first one to 5 points wins! |
| [Build Dust Hopper](tutorials/dust-hopper/index.html) | Scratch tutorial · 8 steps · about 3–4 hours | Dust Hopper is a landing game. You fly a small survey drone over an asteroid and set it down gently on a landing pad. Along the way you will learn how real game physics work, with speed, gravity, thrust and angles, and how to organise a game into small, named pieces. |
| [Lantern Deep](tutorials/lantern-deep/index.html) | Scratch tutorial for older kids (10+) · 7 steps · a few afternoons | Dig down forever. Your lantern only shows the squares right around you: coal, gold, gems, or lava. Collect coins, buy better pickaxes, and watch out for crawlers in the caves. |

Every tutorial folder has `starter.sb3` (artwork, no code) and a `stepN.sb3` for the end of each step.
