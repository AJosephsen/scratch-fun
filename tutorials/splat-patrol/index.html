Splat Patrol

Sneaky raccoon bandits are hiding in the houses, ready to throw water balloons. Windows pop open: splat the raccoons before they splat you, but don't splash the granny or the kid! Round after round the houses change, and the raccoons get quicker.

Every step is a game you can play, and every step has a file you can download if you get stuck. You need the Scratch editor: scratch.mit.edu/projects/editor.

Start here: the starter project has the window with all its costumes, the crosshair, the goo and three houses. No code yet.

Open and close

Big idea: costumes tell the storyThe Window sprite has a costume for every moment: shut, open, and one for everybody who can look out. A forever loop waits a random time, switches to the raccoon, waits, and shuts again. That is already a little play!

Make: nothing new yet. The starter has the Window (with all its costumes), the Crosshair, the Goo and three house backdrops.

Window new
Crosshair new
  • wait (pick random 1 to 3) seconds makes the raccoon hard to predict. Try 0.2 to 0.5 for a very busy raccoon.
  • Look at the Window's costumes: the order matters later. 1 is shut, 2 is open, 3 to 5 are the raccoon, the granny and the kid, then their splashed versions.

Splat!

Big idea: the crosshair gets the clickThe crosshair follows the mouse, so it is always on top of whatever you click. That's why the crosshair gets the click, not the window. It broadcasts splat, and the window checks: is somebody looking out, and am I touching the mouse-pointer? Then they're hit.

Make: the variable score (for all sprites); on the Window who, hit and close for this sprite only; the message splat.

Window changed
Window new
Crosshair changed
Crosshair new
  • who is picked at random: 60 out of 100 times a raccoon, otherwise the granny or the kid. The costume has the same name as who, so switch costume to (who) just works.
  • close is timer + 1.5: the window stays open until the timer passes it, or until hit becomes 1.
  • The splashed costume is join (who) [ splat], like "raccoon splat".
  • There is no wait in the splat script on purpose: a second click would start that script again in the middle of its wait.

Raccoons splat back

Big idea: a timer you can lose toIf a raccoon is still there when its time is up, it throws its water balloon at you: green goo on the screen, and one life gone. Splashing a villager costs a life too. Three lives, then it's game over.

Round after round

Big idea: two numbers make it harderEvery 15 seconds a new round starts, and the houses take turns: the cottage, the shop, the town house, and again. Each round showTime gets shorter and badChance gets bigger. Those two numbers are the whole difficulty.

Make: variables round, house, showTime and badChance; messages clear and build.

Stage new
Window changed
Window new
Window new
Window changed
  • showTime is 2.2 − round × 0.2, but never below 0.7 seconds. badChance is 50 + round × 8, but never above 85.
  • house is (round − 1) mod 3 + 1: 1, 2, 3, 1, 2, 3 … The backdrops are in the same order, so switch backdrop to (house) picks the right house.
  • clear deletes the old clones, build makes the new ones. Each house has its own list of go to and create clone.
  • This is the whole game! Try your own house: draw a backdrop and add a new if house = 4.

Make it your own

  • A golden raccoon that is worth 5 points: add a costume, and a who that is picked once in 20 times.
  • A sound for every splat, and a different one for "Oops!".
  • Your own house: a new backdrop and an if house = 4 with its window spots. Remember to change mod 3 to mod 4.
  • Make it fairer for little brothers and sisters: start showTime at 3 seconds.
